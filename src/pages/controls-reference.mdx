# A CTSM Reference

I'm learning Continuous Time Stochastic Modeling (CTSM) for a research project.
This relies on the Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) to work. In order to ensure I
fully understand it, I'm adding a section to my website breaking everything down
as simply as I can make it. This also serves as a reference for my future self.

The prediction step propagates the estimate through the process model,

$$
\hat{x}_{k|k-1} = f(\hat{x}_{k-1|k-1},\, u_k)
$$

and the covariance follows as $P_{k|k-1} = F_k P_{k-1|k-1} F_k^\top + Q_k$.
